Movie Trivia Site Plan

Joshua Walker

WDD 131-MW 11:30-12:30

Overview

Purpose

This site is to help movie lovers like me learn more about movies and practice trivia questions.

Audience

This is for anyone who loves watching movies or wants to learn more.

Dynamic elements

There will be a search bar that allows users to search The Movie Database which is connected with an API. The results will dynamically display on the page. The child page will have a trivia game that (once started) will randomly choose trivia questions and keep track of points scored.

Questions with answers:

1. In Star Wars: A New Hope, what is the name of Han Solo's iconic ship? A) The Star Destroyer B) The Millennium Falcon C) The Razor Crest

2. Which 1994 Disney movie features a young lion prince named Simba? A) The Jungle Book B) Madagascar C) The Lion King

3. In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, what is the name of Thor's hammer? A) Mjolnir B) Stormbreaker C) Gungnir

4. Which movie is known for the famous line, "I'm going to make him an offer he can't refuse"? A) Goodfellas B) The Godfather C) Scarface

5. In Back to the Future, what speed must the DeLorean reach to travel through time? A) 55 mph B) 88 mph C) 100 mph

6. Who played the character of Jack Sparrow in the Pirates of the Caribbean series? A) Orlando Bloom B) Johnny Depp C) Geoffrey Rush

7. What is the name of the fictional kingdom where Frozen takes place? A) Arendelle B) Genovia C) Florin

8. In The Matrix, what color pill does Neo take to see the truth? A) Blue B) Green C) Red

9. Which actor played the Joker in the 2008 film The Dark Knight? A) Joaquin Phoenix B) Jack Nicholson C) Heath Ledger

10. In the Harry Potter series, what is the name of the school Harry attends? A) Durmstrang Institute B) Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry C) Beauxbatons Academy
